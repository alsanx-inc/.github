# AlsanX

**Industrial Intelligence · Digital Twins · Simulation · AI**

AlsanX builds technology that turns complex industrial operations into **decision-ready digital twins**.

We combine **digital twins, simulation, AI, data analytics, and immersive 3D/XR** to help industrial teams understand their operations, identify bottlenecks, test scenarios, and make better decisions before changes become costly.

### What we do

- 🏭 **Digital Twins** — Build living digital models of plants, production lines, equipment, and logistics systems
- 📊 **Simulation & Optimization** — Analyze capacity, flow, bottlenecks, resources, layouts, and operational scenarios
- 🤖 **Industrial AI** — Apply AI, predictive analytics, and operational intelligence to industrial data
- 👁️ **Computer Vision** — Enable AI-driven quality inspection and operational monitoring
- 🥽 **3D & XR** — Create immersive visualization and operator-training environments
- 🚢 **Industrial & Logistics Solutions** — Support manufacturing, ports, logistics, and complex industrial operations

### Our approach

Every engagement starts with the **operational model**.

We connect **engineering, simulation, AI, visualization, and training** around a digital twin of the real system—creating a shared foundation for analyzing operations, validating scenarios, and improving performance.

### Technology

`AnyLogic` · `anyLogistix` · `Python` · `AI/ML` · `Computer Vision` · `NVIDIA Omniverse` · `3D` · `XR`

### Why AlsanX

Industrial systems are complex. Production flows, equipment, resources, workforce decisions, and constraints interact in ways that are difficult to understand from isolated data or static models.

AlsanX combines **industrial engineering, simulation, digital twins, AI, and immersive technologies** to make that complexity visible, testable, and actionable.
